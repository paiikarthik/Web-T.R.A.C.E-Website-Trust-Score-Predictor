System Architecture & Engineering Specification

Web T.R.A.C.E — System Architecture & Methodology

Core requirements, zero-scraping principles, and research gap solutions governing the Web T.R.A.C.E platform.

1. Zero-Scraping Principle

Traditional phishing detectors fetch web pages and parse HTML, which exposes corporate IP addresses, executes malicious JavaScript, and fails when phishing pages are dead or geo-fenced. Web T.R.A.C.E inspects only the URL lexical and brand structure in <15ms without transmitting HTTP requests to the target host.

2. 3-Tier Operational Risk Stratification

Rather than binary pass/fail, incoming URLs are triaged into:
• Low Risk (Trust ≥65, P <0.35): Benign traffic allowed automatically.
• Medium Risk (Trust 30–65, 0.35 ≤ P <0.70): Triggers step-up authentication or analyst warnings.
• High Risk (Trust <30, P ≥0.70): Immediately blocked and quarantined at the gateway.

3. 10 Benchmarked AI Models + Ensemble

Trained across 5 Machine Learning algorithms (Random Forest, XGBoost, Decision Tree, Logistic Regression, Linear SVM) and 5 Deep Learning architectures (Deep MLP, 1D CNN, Deep LSTM, Hybrid CNN-LSTM, Self-Attention Transformer), plus a Calibrated Soft-Voting Ensemble combining RF, XGBoost, and Deep MLP.

4. User & Administrator Roles

• User: Inspect individual URLs, view Trust Scores, receive plain-English explainability diagnostics.
• Administrator: Monitor benchmark KPIs, execute bulk batch scans, export CSV triage reports, inspect comparative accuracy graphs and ROC/PR curves.
